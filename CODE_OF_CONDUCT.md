# Community guidelines

Restna welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels. Treat people respectfully, give constructive feedback, and focus criticism on code and ideas.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, publishing private information and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable in issues, pull requests or other project spaces.

Maintainers may edit/remove inappropriate content, close or lock discussions, or restrict participation. They should explain actions when appropriate, apply these standards consistently and protect private reports.

Report concerns privately through an available maintainer contact. Until a private contact is configured, request contact without naming affected people or publishing sensitive details in a public issue. Security vulnerabilities follow SECURITY.md. Maintainers should establish a private contact before opening community participation; no private inbox is currently claimed.
